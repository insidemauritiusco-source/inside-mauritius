03 / Dated milestones

FMP's documented 2026 timeline

These dates describe political or parliamentary events and do not constitute an assessment of policy performance or electoral support.

13 April 2026Resignation from MMM

Paul Bérenger, Chetan Baboolall and Joanna Bérenger notified their departure from MMM and intention to sit as an opposition group, as recorded in parliamentary proceedings.

9 May 2026FMP founding congress

Fron Militan Progresis was launched at Vacoas-Phoenix, with three founding parliamentarians.

6 August 2026Opposition leadership

Chetan Baboolall became Leader of the Opposition, according to the National Assembly's official record.

13 August 2026Opposition Whip

The Assembly records Joanna Bérenger as Opposition Whip from this date.

22 October 2026Sydney Pierre clarifies his position

In an interview, he said he had not yet formally joined FMP, although he was sitting alongside its MPs.

24 October 2026FMP confirms a fourth MP

Paul Bérenger announced Sydney Pierre's membership during a party press conference, according to Le Défi.

04 / Election records

Why there is no FMP vote share for 2024

10 Nov 2024Most recent completed National Assembly general election
9 May 2026Launch of the FMP political party

FMP did not exist under its own name at the time of the 2024 general election. The party therefore has no standalone 2024 vote total or electoral seat tally. The MPs listed above were elected in 2024 before the FMP was formed; later parliamentary affiliations are a different kind of statistic from votes won at an election.

Do not report “0% FMP vote share” for 2024. The correct classification is not applicable — did not contest as FMP. No verified nationwide FMP voting-intention survey or election result is included in this report.
