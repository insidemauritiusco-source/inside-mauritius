2024 National Assembly — direct seats

2024 election allocation; these figures are not a claim about current party membership.

Alliance du Changement 60 seatsOrganisation du Peuple de Rodrigues (OPR) 2 seats

62 seats were directly elected: 3 each in mainland constituencies 1–20 and 2 in Rodrigues. Four Best Loser seats were allocated separately. Seat counts should not be confused with national popular vote shares.
