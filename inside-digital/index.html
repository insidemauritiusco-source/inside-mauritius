04 / GOOD TO KNOW

Questions? Answers.

A little clarity goes a long way.

What is Inside Digital? +

Inside Digital is a concept homepage that brings our different digital projects together in one place.

Can I sign in or create an account here? +

Not on this showcase website. Individual apps may have their own account systems; this demo does not collect passwords or user data.

Does the product search work? +

Yes. Search and category filters work directly in your browser to find projects displayed on this page. It is not a web-wide search engine.

Is this website affiliated with Microsoft? +

No. Inside Digital is an independent brand and original website, inspired by clear, modern product showcases.
